Last week, I gave a demo of LingoDB-CT at SIGMOD'25 in Berlin.
As described in the [paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3722212.3725111) in more detail, LingoDB-CT is a instrumentation and visualization framework for LingoDB to help with understanding, debugging, and profiling.
During the well-received demo, I was asked repeatable if the components of LingoDB-CT are open source and can be repurposed for other database systems or other MLIR-based compilers.
This post aims to explain the potential for reusing components for both use-cases and provides a guide for adaptation.
